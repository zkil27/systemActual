# Welcome to Kyna Ops

**Your all-in-one phone app for Kyna Clothing Shop (Shopee PH & TikTok Shop PH)**

Kyna Ops is built so you can run daily orders, stock, and returns straight from your phone—in plain everyday English, without needing a laptop, SKU codes, or BigSeller.

---

## 1. What Kyna Ops Does for You (Store Owner)

### Check Today's Numbers in Seconds (**Today**)
- **Failed Delivery and Buyer Returns stay separate:** You always know which parcels failed delivery and which ones were buyer return/refund requests.
- **See the real picture, not just what the courier says:** For every day (Philippine time), Kyna Ops compares:
  1. **Tagged Today** — parcels tagged as failed delivery or return/refund today.
  2. **Courier Claims Returned** — parcels the courier marked as delivered back to you.
  3. **Actually Scanned Today** — parcels your staff physically scanned at the warehouse.
- **Catch Missing Parcels Early:** If a courier marks a parcel "returned" but your team hasn't scanned it yet, it goes straight to your **Missing** list so you can follow up before the dispute window closes.
- **1-Tap Dispute Messages:** Copy a ready-to-send appeal message for Shopee or TikTok whenever a parcel is missing or damaged.
- **Money Credited Today:** See the cash actually credited for the day at a glance.

### Print Labels & Ship Faster (**Ship**)
- **Wave Printing:** Group ready orders into a wave, preview the count, and print your **pick list first, then all shipping labels** in order on your warehouse thermal printer.
- **Manual or Automatic:** Ship a wave whenever you're ready, or set automatic wave times.
- **One Sticker Per Parcel:** Turn on **Settings → Labels → One sticker per parcel** and big orders no longer spill their Shopee/TikTok label onto a second sticker. The label itself is printed exactly as the shop made it (QR code, barcodes, sort code and address untouched — couriers may reject a changed label). Its overflowing product list is replaced by a **packing slip** sticker printed right after it, which goes **inside** the parcel. Turn it off any time to print the shop's labels as they come.
- **No Double Shipping:** Orders already arranged outside Kyna Ops (or split into multiple parcels) are safely left alone.

### Keep Shopee & TikTok Stock in Sync (**Stock**)
- **No SKUs Needed:** Items are matched by **product photo, item name, and color/size**.
- **Auto-Sync Across Shops:** When an item sells on Shopee, your TikTok stock updates automatically (and vice versa).
- **Safety Buffer:** You can hold back 1–2 pieces from the shops on fast-moving items to avoid overselling.
- **Shopee Auto-Bump:** Star your priority Shopee items to keep them bumped automatically.

### 8:00 PM Daily Summary on Telegram
- Receive a clean daily summary on Telegram every night at **8:00 PM**.
- Get urgent alerts when parcels are missing or damaged.
- Check numbers anytime by sending `/today`, `/failed`, `/returns`, `/missing`, or `/overdue`.

## 2. What Your Warehouse Staff Use (**Scan**)

Your 6 warehouse staff use their own phones (Android or iPhone browser)—no handheld barcode scanners needed.

- **Fast Sign-In:** Staff tap their name and enter their **4-digit PIN**.
- **Scan Returns with Phone Camera:** Scan the waybill barcode when riders drop off returned sacks.
- **Spot Swapped or Damaged Items:** The screen immediately shows the **item photo, color, and size** so staff can confirm the right item is inside and snap a photo if anything is damaged.
- **Log Supplier Deliveries:** Staff can also record **New stock** when fresh items arrive.
- **Private Numbers Stay Private:** Staff never see peso prices, costs, or profit figures.

---

## 3. Switching Over Safely (How We Go Live)

We move your shops in safe steps so nothing gets disrupted:

1. **Connect Your Shops (Read-Only First):** We connect Shopee and TikTok in **Settings** with updates turned *off* first, so you can compare Kyna Ops numbers side-by-side with Seller Center for a day.
2. **Connect Your Warehouse Printer:** We install the small print helper on your shared Windows PC and run a **Test print** to make sure labels come out cleanly.
3. **Sync Starting Stock:** We pull your current items and stock from both shops and double-check any unmatched variations.
4. **Test 5 Parcels:** We turn off BigSeller, turn on stock sync, and run a small **5-parcel test wave** to confirm labels print and couriers book pickup normally.
5. **Go Live:** Once the 5-parcel test looks good, you're ready for full daily waves.

> **Safety Net:** If you ever want to pause shipping or stock updates, you can turn off the switches in **Settings** at any time—your returns tracking and Telegram summaries will still keep working.
